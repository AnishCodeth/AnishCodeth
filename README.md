## 🧑‍💻 About

I'm a **Backend Engineer at [Yarsa Labs](https://yarsalabs.com)**, where I design and ship production services for gaming and fintech/IoT products. I also do **NLP and applied ML research**, and I'm interested in how computation can help with water and energy problems.

- 🎮 Built multi-tenant gaming backends with real-time leaderboards, OAuth2/OIDC auth, in-app purchase validation and async pipelines
- 🔔 Built an IoT payment-alert service (bank webhooks → signature verification → MQTT audio alerts to merchant devices)
- 🛍️ Built a recommendation system that predicts what customers are likely to buy
- 📄 Co-authored **NepGradeQA**, a Nepali short-answer grading dataset, at ICTP 2026
- 🌊 Researching **floating photovoltaics** and integrated water–energy assessment
- 🏅 Licensed Professional Engineer (Nepal Engineering Council) · Hackathon 2nd runner-up
